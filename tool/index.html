Flow Composer Help Guide

What is Flow Composer?

Flow Composer is a tool for creating and managing flows through archival materials. It allows you to search archives, explore materials, and create custom "flows" that trace specific themes through time and space.

How to Search

1. Choose Your Search Scope

  • Collection: Search within the collection only
  • Entire Archive: Search across all available materials in the Internet Archive

2. Enter Search Terms

  • Try keywords related to your research topic
  • Combine terms for more specific results
  • Use relevant terminology for your field of study

4. Filter Results

  • Document Type: Filter by texts, images, audio, or videos
  • Results per page: Choose how many items to display (6-48)

Exploring Materials

Viewing Material Details

  • Click any material card to view detailed information in the right panel
  • See metadata including title, description, creator, date, and file size
  • Use pagination controls at top and bottom to navigate through results

Fullscreen Media Experience

  • Open Fullscreen: Click "Open Fullscreen" to view media in a larger window
  • Fullscreen Modal: Opens a dedicated fullscreen window for better viewing
  • Multiple Media Types: Supports videos, audio, documents, and images in fullscreen
  • Easy Navigation: Use the close button (×) or press Escape to exit fullscreen
  • Direct Archive Links: Quick access to view materials on Internet Archive

Selecting Materials

Building Your Collection

  • Click "+ Select" on any material card to add it to your selection
  • Selected materials appear in the "Selected Materials" section below
  • Use "Clear All" to remove all selections and start over
  • You need at least one material selected to create a flow

Creating Flows

What are Flows?

Flows are thematic pathways through archival materials. They organize materials into standardized document types:

  1. Photographic Documentation - Visual evidence and imagery
  2. Conversational Documentation - Oral histories and interviews
  3. Endangered Documents - At-risk materials and ephemera
  4. Academic Documents - Research and scholarly analysis
  5. Policy Documents - Legislation and regulations
  6. Financial Documents - Economic and funding information
  7. Ephemeral Web Documents - Online content and digital media
  8. Institutional Documents - Official records and reports

Creating Your Flow

  • Click "Create Flow from Archival Materials" when you have selected materials
  • Name your flow with a descriptive title
  • Add a description explaining the flow's focus
  • Assign materials to document types - categorize each material into one of the 8 standardized types
  • Reorder materials by dragging them to create your desired narrative flow

Material Organization

  • Drag Handle: Use the ⋮⋮ icon to drag materials and reorder them
  • Document Type Assignment: Assign each material to one of the 8 standardized document types
  • Research Notes: Add specific notes for each material
  • Coverage Analysis: See which document types are covered in your flow

Managing Your Flows

Viewing & Editing Flows

  • Created flows appear in the "Created Flows" section
  • Click on any flow to view detailed information
  • Edit flows to modify materials, document types, and order
  • Search and filter your flows by name or content

Exporting and Importing

  • Export flows to share with colleagues or backup your work
  • Import flows from JSON files
  • Automatic saving - flows are saved to your browser automatically
